About FileFlux

Built for students, professionals, and everyone who just needs to convert a file — fast, free, and without uploading anything to the cloud.

What is FileFlux?

FileFlux is a free online file conversion tool that runs entirely inside your web browser. There are no servers, no signups, and no subscriptions. Every conversion — whether you're turning a PDF into images, merging documents, or compressing a photo — happens locally on your device using modern browser APIs.

Why We Built This

Most file converter websites make you upload your sensitive documents to a remote server. This raises real privacy concerns — especially for students sharing academic work, office workers handling business documents, or anyone dealing with personal files. FileFlux solves this by doing all processing client-side, so your files never leave your device.

Who Is This For?

FileFlux was designed for:

  • College students who need to convert assignment files quickly
  • Office professionals working with PDFs and images daily
  • Small business owners who need simple, free document tools
  • Anyone who values privacy and doesn't want to sign up for yet another service

The Technology

FileFlux uses powerful open-source JavaScript libraries including PDF.js (by Mozilla), pdf-lib, jsPDF, JSZip, and browser-image-compression — all running directly in your browser at full speed, with no server involved.

Is It Really Free?

Yes — completely free, with no hidden limits. FileFlux is supported by non-intrusive advertising (Google AdSense). You never need to pay, subscribe, or create an account to use any tool on this site.
