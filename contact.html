Frequently Asked Questions

Are my files really private?

Yes, 100%. All processing happens inside your browser using JavaScript. We have absolutely no server-side component, so your files never travel over the internet to us. Not even a single byte.

Why is my browser slow during conversion?

Large PDF files or high-resolution images can use significant CPU/RAM during conversion because everything runs locally. Try using a desktop browser for large files, or lower the quality/scale settings.

Can I use this on mobile?

Yes! FileFlux works on Android and iOS browsers. For very large files, a desktop browser is recommended for best performance.
