Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2024. We keep things simple — your files stay on your device, always.

1. Overview

FileFlux ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our website at fileflux.github.io (or your custom domain).

The short version: Your files are never uploaded to our servers. All file processing happens locally inside your web browser. We have no access to your files whatsoever.

2. Files and Data You Process

All file conversion, compression, and processing performed on FileFlux occurs entirely within your web browser using client-side JavaScript. Your files are:

  • Never uploaded to FileFlux servers or any third-party servers
  • Never stored anywhere outside your device
  • Never transmitted over the internet
  • Immediately discarded from browser memory after you close or navigate away

3. Information We Collect Automatically

Like most websites, we collect limited technical information automatically through standard web logs and analytics. This may include:

  • Your browser type and version
  • Your operating system
  • Pages you visit on our site and time spent
  • Referring website (how you found us)
  • General geographic location (country/city — not precise address)

This information is used only to understand how our site is used and to improve it. It is not linked to your identity.

4. Google AdSense and Cookies

FileFlux displays ads served by Google AdSense. Google may use cookies and similar tracking technologies to display ads relevant to your interests. Google's use of advertising cookies is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings. You can also opt out of third-party vendor cookies by visiting the Network Advertising Initiative opt-out page.

5. Google Analytics

We may use Google Analytics to understand site traffic. Google Analytics uses cookies to collect anonymised data about usage. You can opt out using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

6. Cookies

Our own website does not set any functional cookies. Third-party services (Google AdSense, Google Analytics) may set cookies on your device. You can control cookie settings through your browser preferences.

7. Children's Privacy

FileFlux does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us immediately.

8. Links to Other Websites

Our site may contain links to external websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those websites and encourage you to read their privacy policies.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Your continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance of the new policy.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through our Contact page.
